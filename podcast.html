What The Signal is

A done-for-you podcast launch built around your business.

Most people get stuck before the first episode. Between microphones, hosting, branding, Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube, editing, and launch planning, there are dozens of decisions to make before you ever hit record.

That's the part I take care of. Over six to eight weeks, we plan your show, brand it, set up the tech, and launch it, so you can focus on the part only you can do: having conversations worth listening to. Done well, your podcast becomes the place future clients get to know you before they ever book a call.

Podcast launch questions

How much does it cost to launch a podcast with you?

The Signal is $1,297 for the whole launch: show planning, cover art and branding, hosting and directory setup on Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and the other major platforms, a launch plan, and the workflows to keep publishing. Ongoing production after launch is optional and priced separately.

Do I need any podcast experience?

Not at all. Most of my clients are starting from scratch. I'll guide you through every decision, explain the process, and handle the technical side so you can focus on sharing your expertise.

How long does it take to launch a podcast?

Most podcast launches are completed in about six to eight weeks, though timelines can vary slightly depending on branding, scheduling, and how quickly we move through feedback.

Do I need expensive equipment?

No. You can sound great without expensive gear. I'll recommend equipment that fits your budget and help you set it up.

Who owns everything when we're finished?

You do. Your podcast, artwork, hosting accounts, branding, workflows, templates, and systems all belong to you. Nothing lives in my accounts.

What happens after we launch?

Once your podcast is live, you'll have everything you need to keep publishing confidently on your own. If you'd rather stay focused on your business, you can also continue with ongoing production support.
