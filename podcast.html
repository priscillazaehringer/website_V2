The Signal

There's more to launching a podcast than recording your first episode.

What are you going to talk about? How will the podcast support your business? Where will you host it? And how will you find the time to keep producing episodes alongside everything else you're already doing?

These are the things we'll work through together.

I'll take care of the planning, branding, technical setup, and launch. We'll also establish a production process that works with your schedule, your team, and the way you already run your business.

The goal is to give potential clients another way to discover your business, learn from you, and get to know how you work before they ever reach out.

What a podcast does for your business

  • Get found

    A new way for people to discover you, through podcast apps, search, and the guests you bring on.

  • Build trust before the call

    An hour of listening does what a sales call can't. Prospects show up already knowing your expertise and how you work.

  • Feed your other marketing

    Every episode becomes emails, social posts, and blog content, so one recording supports several channels.

The Signal has two parts: the launch we build together, and the production process you keep afterward.

Side A · The buildYour 6–8 week launch

Here's what we'll work on together.

  1. 01

    Podcast strategy

    Weeks 1–2

    We'll define your audience, podcast name, topics, format, publishing frequency, and how the show fits into your broader marketing strategy.

  2. 02

    Cover art & branding

    Weeks 2–4

    I'll create your podcast cover art and visual assets that complement your existing business branding.

  3. 03

    Hosting & technical setup

    Weeks 3–6

    I'll set up your podcast hosting, RSS feed, and distribution to Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and other major podcast directories. We'll also work through your equipment, recording setup, and production process.

  4. 04

    Launch preparation

    Weeks 6–8

    I'll put together your launch schedule, promotional assets, and announcement emails, and coordinate getting your podcast live.

Podcast launch questions

How much does it cost to launch a podcast with you?

The Signal is $1,297 for the whole launch: show planning, cover art and branding, hosting and directory setup on Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and the other major platforms, a launch plan, and the workflows to keep publishing. Ongoing production after launch is optional and priced separately.

Do I need any podcast experience?

Not at all. Most of my clients are starting from scratch. I'll guide you through every decision, explain the process, and handle the technical side so you can focus on sharing your expertise.

How long does it take to launch a podcast?

Most podcast launches are completed in about six to eight weeks, though timelines can vary slightly depending on branding, scheduling, and how quickly we move through feedback.

Do I need expensive equipment?

No. You can sound great without expensive gear. I'll recommend equipment that fits your budget and help you set it up.

Who owns everything when we're finished?

You do. Your podcast, artwork, hosting accounts, branding, workflows, templates, and systems all belong to you. Nothing lives in my accounts.

What happens after we launch?

Once your podcast is live, you'll have everything you need to keep publishing confidently on your own. If you'd rather stay focused on your business, you can also continue with ongoing production support.
