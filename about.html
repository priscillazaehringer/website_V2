Hi, I'm Priscilla.

I'm a marketing and operations person from Iowa, which is a long way of saying I help small business owners plan their marketing and then actually get it built.

How I got here

I was born and raised in Iowa, and I'm still here. I'm a mom of three, and I'd much rather have an honest conversation with you than give you a polished pitch.

I earned my MBA in 2020, right in the middle of the pandemic, with all three kids at home. Yes, it was exactly as busy as it sounds.

After that I tried a little of everything: operations, marketing, content, systems. I worked as an online business manager, which is how I got into podcasts. I started out editing episodes, and before long I was planning launches, coordinating guests, and building the workflows behind the shows. Funnels, email, and running ads for clients came the same way.

Today that's my business. I do ongoing marketing support for a small group of clients, podcast launches, and one-off projects when someone needs one thing done. You can see how that works here.

  • 6+Years doing this work
  • 40+Meta campaigns managed
  • 300+Podcast episodes produced
  • 30+Funnels & systems built

What working with me is like

I'm probably the person asking why your email automation is doing that weird thing, or reminding you that we need to finalize the landing page before the ads go live.

And yes, I'm also the person making sure those things actually get done.

I like details. I like figuring things out. And I really like when a client can hand something over and know it's taken care of.

I keep my client list small on purpose, so you're always working with me. I'll also tell you if I don't think you're ready for something yet. I'd rather say that up front than take your money for something that won't work.

The hills I'll die on

  • Your podcast is a marketing tool, not a revenue stream.

    Sponsorships are nice, but the real value is that people get to know you before they ever book a call.

  • Done and live beats perfect and stuck.

    A good-enough funnel that's live this month will teach you more than a perfect one still sitting in a Google Doc.

  • Small businesses deserve good help too.

    A lot of good marketing help is priced for companies with a whole marketing department. I'd like the owner who's doing it all themselves to have someone in their corner, too.

When I'm not working

I'm usually off on some kind of adventure. That might be a weekend trip, a Vikings or Hawkeyes game, or cheering on my oldest, who's a traveling chalk artist.

I love wandering vintage stores, and my day doesn't really start until I've made my homemade latte. My TV taste runs from TLC to nerdier favorites like Game of Thrones, sometimes in the same night. And if I weren't doing this, I'd be a teacher. It was my first major.
