Hi, I'm Priscilla.

I'm a marketing and operations person from Iowa, which is a long way of saying I help small business owners plan their marketing and then actually get it built.

How I got here

Since 2020, I've been working behind the scenes of other people's businesses, starting as an Online Business Manager. It didn't take long to realize how much I enjoyed it.

That's also how I got into podcasting. I started by editing episodes and eventually took on everything from planning podcast launches and coordinating guests to managing ongoing production. Over the years, my work expanded into email marketing, building funnels, managing Meta ads, and creating systems that made life a little easier for my clients.

What I love most about my work is the variety. I get to be both creative and analytical, work with some really great people, and help bring their ideas to life.

Today, I work closely with a small group of business owners, providing ongoing marketing and operational support, done-for-you podcast launches, and individual projects.

I genuinely enjoy getting to know the people I work with, understanding their businesses, and becoming someone they can count on.

  • 6+Years doing this work
  • 40+Meta campaigns managed
  • 300+Podcast episodes produced
  • 30+Funnels & systems built

What working with me is like

I'm naturally a curious person. I like knowing how things work, figuring out why something isn't working, and finding better ways to do things. I'm also pretty detail-oriented, which comes in handy with the kind of work I do.

One of my favorite things about working with business owners is being able to take something off their plate so they don't have to think about it anymore.

I intentionally keep my client list small because I want to have the time to really get to know you, your business, and how you like to work. When you hire me, you're working directly with me.

I'm also a big believer in open and honest communication. If I don't think something makes sense for your business right now, I'll tell you. I'm mindful of the money you're investing in your business, and I want you to feel good about where it's going.

And while I take my work seriously, I don't think everything needs to be so serious. I enjoy getting to know my clients as people, sharing a good laugh, and celebrating the little wins along the way.

The hills I'll die on

  • Your podcast is a marketing tool, not a revenue stream.

    Sponsorships are nice, but the real value is that people get to know you before they ever book a call.

  • Done and live beats perfect and stuck.

    A good-enough funnel that's live this month will teach you more than a perfect one still sitting in a Google Doc.

  • Small businesses deserve good help too.

    A lot of good marketing help is priced for companies with a whole marketing department. I'd like the owner who's doing it all themselves to have someone in their corner, too.
