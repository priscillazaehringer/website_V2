Who this is for

You've got an offer that sells and an audience that trusts you. What you don't have is someone who can look at all of your marketing, decide what matters this month, and then go build it.

Maybe you've hired a strategist who handed you a beautiful plan you never had time to carry out. Or a VA who needed you to explain every step. Either way, you're still the one holding it all together.

That's where I come in. I help you decide what to focus on, and then I'm the one who does it.

The roles I fill

Most businesses would hire a different person for each of these. Depending on the month, I'm one of them, or all six.

Marketing strategist

Deciding what to focus on

So you're working on the right things, not just the loudest ones.

I look at your offers, audience, and calendar, and help you decide what to launch, what to fix, and what to drop. For one agency client, that meant planning a holiday offer, a weekly webinar series, and a podcast series around one launch calendar.

Project manager

Keeping every moving part on track

So deadlines get hit and nothing waits on a missing reply.

Timelines, handoffs, and who owes what. For a funnel agency, I built a roadmap page for every client, so each one always knew their next step and when it was due.

Ads manager

Running Meta ads that build your list

So new leads come in every day, not just on a good social media week.

40+ Meta campaigns managed. One client's $5-a-day campaign brought in about seven new subscribers a day, at around 70 cents each.

Podcast producer

Getting your show out every week

So your podcast keeps publishing, even when you're busy.

300+ episodes produced for business, marketing, and healthcare shows, including client podcasts ranked in the top 10% and top 2.5%. See The Signal.

Website & funnel developer

Building pages that work

So your pages look good, work right, and don't need babysitting.

30+ funnels and systems built: sales pages, landing pages, websites, and client portals, coded by me. Recent builds include sales pages that close their own launch deadlines.

Copyeditor

Polishing what goes out

So everything sounds like you, and nothing goes out with a typo.

Sales pages, emails, show notes, and social posts. I tighten the copy, check it against your voice, and catch mistakes before your audience does.

Questions people usually ask

What is a fractional CMO?

A part-time marketing lead. You get someone who decides what your marketing should focus on, without hiring a full-time executive. With me, that also includes building what we plan: the funnels, emails, and launches.

Are you a fractional CMO, an online business manager, or a VA?

A bit of the first two. I help decide what to do, like a fractional CMO, and I handle the marketing operations behind it, like an online business manager. I'm not a VA, and I'm not trying to run your company. You're still the CEO.

How many hours do I need?

It depends on what's on your plate. Tell me what you want done over the next few months, and I'll suggest a monthly block from there.

Can I start with one project?

Yes. A lot of people start with one project, like a funnel or an email sequence, and decide about ongoing support after that.

Do you work in my tools?

Yes. I work inside your email platform, project management tool, and accounts, so everything I build stays yours.

How much does a fractional CMO cost?

Ongoing support is a monthly block of hours, and projects are quoted up front. Send me what you're working on and I'll give you a real number, not a range.
